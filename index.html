The company

Built by VertiPI Labs to make movement intelligent.

Gyms and wellness spaces are evolving from equipment-driven rooms into intelligent, data-driven ecosystems. Wearables improved biometric tracking, but the actual quality of human movement inside these spaces stays largely unmeasured and invisible.

Sthira OS is not another fitness app. It is an infrastructure layer built on computer vision, biomechanics and skeletal mapping that analyzes movement in real time and delivers actionable insight to members and trainers alike.

Privacy by design

Intelligence that respects the people in the room.

Here's how the data actually flows. Every step runs on a Sthira OS edge device inside your facility, so the data never leaves the premises or the hardware.

1

Capture on-site

An in-room camera streams directly to the Sthira OS edge device installed at the facility, not to the cloud.

→
2

Processed on the edge device

Movement is analyzed in real time on the device itself. The data never leaves the premises or the hardware.

→
3

Signals kept, video discarded

The device extracts skeletal and biomechanical signals like reps, posture and symmetry, then discards the underlying video. We keep the movement data, not the recording.
