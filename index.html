The company

Built by VertiPI Labs to make movement intelligent.

Gyms and wellness spaces are evolving from equipment-driven rooms into intelligent, data-driven ecosystems. Wearables improved biometric tracking — but the actual quality of human movement inside these spaces remains largely unmeasured and invisible.

Sthira OS is not another fitness app. It is an infrastructure layer built on computer vision, biomechanics and skeletal mapping that analyzes movement in real time and delivers actionable insight to members and trainers alike.

What premium operators actually care about.
  • Trainer efficiency and augmentation
  • Member engagement and retention
  • Measurable movement quality
  • A genuinely premium experience
  • Workout intelligence at scale
